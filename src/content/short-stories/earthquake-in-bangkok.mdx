I was sitting in burger king, having just ordered some french fries, waiting at a table. It was a very small little area that people wait in and a long hallway that went back to the dining area. There was a couple eating food next to me. I felt the Earth shake a little bit, looked around and the woman was looking up in the air, the way you look around if you hear a weird noise and you're trying to figure out what it is. Then the Earth shook again, and me and her made eye contact and she did the move you do when you're trying to ask someone whether they felt the Earth move. At the same time, he put his sandwich down and started looking around. Then we all walked briskly outside. A guy was walking with his headphones on, he stopped, looked at me and did the move you do when you're asking someone else whether or not the Earth just shook. I nodded and he nodded and kept walking. Then I looked around and people were running and screaming from both ways. People were getting out of their cars and running in whichever direction. I went down the side street, people in bars were sitting there pointing up at something in the sky which I later determined was buildings swaying. I walked the other way, and when I got back to Sukhimvit the Earth shook again, so I determined the bad stuff was happening on Sukhimvit and I wasn't going to go that way again. I just kept pacing up and down the side street and then decided I needed a beer, so I stopped at an Irish pub. 

The bartender looked terrified but was doing a good job being friendly and comforting, as bartenders do. I ordered a big draft beer and sat down outside. She seemed to think that was a good place to sit, rather than inside. I heard a dude say "Ohio" and went over to speak in English to somebody. It was 2 dudes and a girl sitting at a table and I sat with them for a while. One dude was from Columbus, one dude was from Detroit, and the woman was from Finland. After a bit of talking, the dudes were in Bangkok for 1 day on business and she lived there. I asked what they did, they said they work for the Department of Homeland Security out of the Columbus office. The dude from Detroit was on his second beer and visibly more shook by the ordeal. He was like my size but jacked and with a bunch of tattoos. He was around the same age as me, and the dude from Columbus was probably 5-10 years older. He was taller, thinner, and more collected. After some more conversation he turned out to be the team lead. They were partners, working for some office under DHS as agents catching sex predators in Thailand. Their work had picked up significantly since the administration change. The dude from Detroit was fired up about that, seemingly sure that they were out catching bad guys and that's what he signed up for. The guy from Columbus was less drunk, more reserved, but I kinda got the feeling he was less enthusiastic. Maybe in the way everyone is the longer they've been doing their job, or maybe for other reasons. The Finnish woman was kinda mocking them playfully the whole time and seemed to have a lot of knowledge about world politics and definitely was very liberal. "Big bad American soldiers and they don't even have a plan in place for an earth quake" kind of vibes. She somehow walked an unbelievably fine line of not pissing them off, but trashing them and their work pretty heavily. I offered to buy everyone another beer. The Detroit guy, Mike, took one, the dude from Columbus, Joe, was empty but declined, and the woman was still full. When I came back outside, Joe was on the phone around the corner and Mike volunteered that Joe was "probably talking to our 'attache', letting them know we're alive". The attache had called Mike first, but he didn't answer "obviously". I didn't know what he meant by that but it was clear that Mike really enjoyed saying "attache", and i was pretty over-the-moon excited that I knew the word because of my love affair with the TV show Patriot. He used the word at least a couple more times, and I egged him on as best I could, trying to see if he was making a Patriot joke or if he was serious, without saying anything about the show. I'm pretty sure he was serious.

When Joe got off the phone, Mike asked if that was the attache, Joe said no, and then Mike gave me some more details about their operation. I asked him how he ended up at DHS. He joined the army after 9/11, angry as hell that someone would attack the US like that, spent time in Iraq and Kuwait fighting terrorists, and then joined the border patrol when he was done. He had tried to join back up in the Army a few years ago, but they "didn't want used guys with PTSD" and turned him down. 

Some random details from throughout these conversations with mostly Mike:
- They were trying to figure out if their flight was canceled, it was scheduled for 7:00pm
- They fly commercial and usually buy out the back 20 seats of the plane or so
- When they have a prisoner, they have 2 agents for each prisoner - but lately this rule hasn't been being following and the 2 of them had 3 prisoners on their last flight. I think they dropped the the prisoners off in Patong? and flew to bangkok, stayed one night and were flying to hong kong then to the States from there
- They were staying right by the airport and their hotel was evacuated
- They didn't know about grab

Eventually Joe and Mike left, I bought the Finnish woman a beer and asked her what she did. She worked for the UN in some capacity and her work right now heavily involved helping get food and other aide to victims of the civil war in Myanmar. Her husband and 2 kids were back at their apartment in Bangkok, and she was staying at a nearby hotel for a few days because the rest of her family was sick and they were worried it might be COVID. She had lived in Japan for 5 years and so had been through some earthquakes but she said nothing like this one.
