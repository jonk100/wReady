# Apple Corps: Freedom as New Cage

The Beatles' naive bid for artistic and business control creates a new bureaucracy that becomes another pressure system and vulnerability surface for external manipulation.

## Key Elements

### Formation Optimism
- **"Western communism":** Initial vision of creative freedom and collective ownership
- **Business naivete:** Artists attempting to run business without experience or discipline
- **Escape from record labels:** Desire for control that ironically creates new constraints

### Bureaucratic Reality
- **Sprawl and chaos:** Apple grows beyond the band's capacity to manage effectively
- **Financial pressures:** The need for revenue creates artistic compromises
- **Infiltration surface:** Complex organization provides access points for external manipulation

### Symbolic Function
- **Boardroom setting:** The final dissolution occurs in Apple's boardroom, not creative space
- **Legal language:** The band's end is articulated through corporate documents
- **Institutional irony:** The attempt to escape institutions ends in institutional dissolution

## Relationship to Conspiracy

- **Vulnerability:** Apple's complexity provides cover for covert operations
- **Justification:** The band's business difficulties are cited as evidence of chaos requiring intervention
- **Setting:** Apple offices become locations for clandestine meetings and operations

## Relationship to Other Plots

- **Enables:** The Crown's Covert War (A-plot) by providing operational cover
- **Accelerates:** Beatles Creative Fracture (E-subplot) through business pressures
- **Contrasts with:** The creative freedom the band sought to achieve
