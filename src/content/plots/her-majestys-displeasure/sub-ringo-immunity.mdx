# Ringo: The Uncorruptible Control Group

Ringo Starr's role as the emotional baseline who remains essentially human while everyone else descends into obsession, ambition, or delusion.

## Key Elements

### Immunity to Manipulation
- **Pub locals:** Ringo's normal life and connections make him psychologically grounded
- **Poisoned tea gag:** The operation's attempt to manipulate Ringo fails because contentment can't be manufactured
- **Registry payoff:** Ringo's MI5 file is blank, confirming his immunity to psychological operations

### Emotional Barometer Function
- **Canary in the coal mine:** Ringo's repeated phrases change meaning as the band deteriorates
- **Stabilizing presence:** His steadiness provides contrast to John/Paul tensions and George's withdrawal
- **Human scale:** Ringo represents ordinary values within extraordinary circumstances

### Later-Life Resolution
- **Film set:** Continues working and living normally after the band's dissolution
- **Dissolution notice:** Reads the legal end with practical acceptance, not drama
- **Survival:** Of all Beatles, Ringo is the least damaged by the experience

## Symbolic Function

- **Control group:** Demonstrates that the conspiracy's effects are selective, not universal
- **Institutional failure:** Proves that not everyone can be psychologically manipulated
- **Authenticity:** Ringo's lack of artistic ambition makes him immune to the pressures that destroy others

## Relationship to Other Plots

- **Resists:** The Crown's Covert War (A-plot) and Operatives' Divergence (B-plot)
- **Measures:** Beatles Creative Fracture (E-subplot) through emotional stability
- **Contrasts with:** John Lennon's Radicalization (C-plot) in response to pressure
