There was a ton of people at my parents' house having a party. I didn't know most of them, but I know Annie was there because I was looking for her the whole time, and Ursula was there because she kept telling me everything was cool and not to worry. We were chillin' mostly in the Florida room, and the sky and the moon were huge, bright, colorful, and beautiful. At some point, I realized there were a bunch of people in my parents' bathroom and got mad and tried to make everyone leave, but there were tons of people there and it took forever. Then I was outside with a group of people looking at the big, beautiful sky, which had some green clouds forming. For some reason, I knew the green clouds were bad, but everyone else thought they just looked beautiful and amazing. Then they started to fall.
