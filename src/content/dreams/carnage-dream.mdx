I was working for a company where we drove around in a bus, I think that was the whole job. We were going to get the bus wrapped in a plaza that included Hines and an eyeglass factory. I hopped out and starting taking to the people who work at the eyeglass factory, who were on break. They said working there sucked. One of them was a guy I was in jail with who I don't really remember very well. Everybody was smoking cigs, it was like 10-15 of us. 

Then I saw the work bus backing up out of the place they pulled in and I thought they were leaving me. That was fine. But they were just moving to another spot. Then we were in the bus getting wrapped. It was like being in a wormhole or a car wash. Then we were all out of the bus talking, looking at the bus. Then we saw dudes with guns pointing them at the bus from afar.  We realized there were terrorists in the bus so we started crawling away and saw carnage everywhere.

* The dude I used I work with at the gas station that I never got his name was driving a white limo that had been in a couple car crashes that I had witnessed recently. Some dude I was working with now said something like,

"he'd never get in a crash" 

..and I was like,

"no, I saw him get in like at least 2 recent carcrashes."

Then the guy I used to work with had a gun and was one of the cia agents or whatever they were who were surrounding the bus.
