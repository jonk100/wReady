I was at the house, but it was way bigger. Annie was at school. I saw a really tiny, fluffy, orange little kitty under the bed, and I thought it was going to run if I went up to it, but when I did, it came up and rubbed against me. I heard a meow that sounded like Bitty, and I turned around and saw a small white and orange kitty with two little baby black and orange kitties that looked like twins. I saw that it was 1 o'clock. Then on the stage, I saw a huge, multi-colored cat. It looked like a panther, but it was black and purple and green and blue and glowed in the dark, and it was super wild-looking. There were a bunch of other cats I don't remember, but they were all really nice. I was kinda worried, though, that I shouldn't let them all in the house. Then Annie called me; she needed picked up from work. She said she'd been calling since one, and I looked at the time, and I had been meeting new cats for an hour because it was 2 o'clock now. She was very nice about it, though—it was no problem, and she was getting out early.
