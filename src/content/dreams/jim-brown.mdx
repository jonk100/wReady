Jim Brown was singing and dancing at some sort of party. He was elegantly dressed in a tuxedo and he was the star of the show. I walked by, gave him a fist bump, and walked down a hallway to another thing. A gay old black guy that used to come into the BP in an orange suit was there in an orange suit. I told him Jim Brown was playing over that way. He said something about how he knew he was playing over there, and insinuated that he was boys with Jim Brown. I was boys with Jim Brown though, and I kinda got the feeling he was full of shit.
