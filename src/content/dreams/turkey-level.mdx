I was at a new job in an office. Everyone was asleep at their desks. I was working, doing good, etc. Then I fell asleep, and when I woke up I was the only one in the office. Somebody came and told me we were doing some stuff in another room. An HR guy told me that I'm doing a pretty good job, but I can't be falling asleep at my desk. Right now, I was on "Turkey Level" and I'm ok, but if I get below a 60, that's when I need to worry. I didn't know what number was associated with Turkey Level but they gave me a cool sticker.
